Lesson 06

Rhythm & Intonation

French sounds different from English even when every individual sound is right — because of rhythm (how syllables are timed) and intonation (how pitch rises and falls).

Lesson Progress 0%

01 · Learn

Every syllable gets its turn.

English is stress-timed — stressed syllables get more time and unstressed ones get swallowed. French is syllable-timed — each syllable gets roughly equal length, giving French its steady, even rhythm.

🎵

Listen for the rhythm

Notice how each syllable in il fait beau gets about the same length, instead of one syllable jumping out.

05 · Sentence

Now use it naturally.

A short back-and-forth: a rising question, then a falling answer. Read both slowly, then at a natural pace.

Tu viens ce soir ? — Oui, je viens.

Pronunciation Tip

Don't stress individual words the way you would in English. Let the pitch of the whole sentence do the work instead.
