Lesson 04

Silent Letters

French spelling keeps a lot of letters that aren't pronounced. Learn the patterns so you stop guessing.

Lesson Progress 0%

01 · Learn

Remember: CaReFuL.

Most final consonants in French are silent. The exceptions are C, R, F, L — easy to remember as the word “CaReFuL”. Those four usually are pronounced at the end of a word.

C·R·F·L

Listen for the difference

chef ends in F — one of the CaReFuL letters — so it's pronounced. nid (nest) ends in D, which is silent.

Pronunciation Tip

When in doubt, say fewer letters, not more. English speakers tend to over-pronounce French words — French tends to simplify.
