Lesson 03

Nasal Sounds

French has four nasal vowels — sounds like an, in, on, and un — where air escapes through the nose as well as the mouth.

Lesson Progress 0%

01 · Learn

It's about where the air goes.

For a nasal vowel, the soft palate at the back of the mouth (the velum) drops down, opening a path for air into the nose. The tongue position barely changes from the vowel's non-nasal cousin — the airflow is what's different.

on

Listen for the difference

bon (good) ends in a nasal vowel — the "n" isn't fully pronounced, the air just goes through the nose. bonne (good, feminine) pronounces the "n" fully instead, which blocks the nasal sound.

05 · Sentence

Now use it naturally.

This sentence uses all four nasal vowels. Read it slowly, then try it at a natural pace.

Le bon vin est dans un coin.

Pronunciation Tip

Don't pronounce the "n" itself in these words — it's just there to signal that the vowel before it is nasal.
