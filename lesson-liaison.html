Lesson 05

Liaison

French words link together in connected speech. A silent final consonant can suddenly be pronounced — if the next word starts with a vowel sound.

Lesson Progress 0%

01 · Learn

Two words become one sound.

Say les on its own and the S is silent. Say les right before amis, and that same S turns into a "z" sound that glues the two words together: le-z-ami.

z

Listen for the difference

les alone has no S sound. les amis together does — that's liaison.

Not every possible liaison is actually made in casual speech — native speakers skip some of them. When in doubt, these five patterns are the most reliable ones to use.
