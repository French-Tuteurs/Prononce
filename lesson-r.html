The French R

Learn how to recognize, produce, and practice one of French's most distinctive sounds.

Lesson Progress 0%

01 · Learn

What is the French R?

The French R is made farther back in your mouth than the English R. Instead of curling your tongue, let the sound come from the back of your mouth.

R

Listen to the sound

Take a moment to listen carefully before trying it yourself.

02 · Position

Find the right position.

Keep the front of your tongue relaxed. The important movement happens toward the back of your mouth.
