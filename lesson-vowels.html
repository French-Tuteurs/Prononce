Lesson 01

French Vowels

French has more vowel sounds than English — ten distinct ones. Learn what changes between them, and practice each one.

Lesson Progress 0%

01 · Learn

Three things change between vowels.

Every French vowel comes down to three things: how high the tongue is, how far forward or back it sits, and whether the lips are rounded. Change any one of those and you get a different vowel.

i·u

Listen for the difference

Both use the same tongue position — high and forward. The only difference is the lips: spread for i, rounded for u (written “u” in French, as in tu).

05 · Sentence

Now use it naturally.

Read the sentence slowly, then try saying it at a natural pace. It uses several of the vowels you just practiced.

Tu as bu de l'eau.

Pronunciation Tip

French vowels stay pure — they don't glide into a second sound the way many English vowels do. Keep the tongue and lips steady for the whole vowel.
